Our team

Six students. One shared brief: build something that actually helps.

Every role below reflects the primary focus each member brought to SignSight — most of us contributed across research, design and build.

NF

Namweda Ferdinard

Team Lead & Systems Design

Coordinates the team and holds the overall concept together — from the hardware layout to how the pieces of the pitch fit.

BC

Braga Charles

Hardware & Industrial Design

Leads thinking on the physical form factor — camera placement, weight distribution, and how the glasses should look and feel on a wearer's face.

NA

Tjinae Jorokee

AI & Gesture Recognition

Focuses on how a lightweight, on-device model could realistically recognise a small sign vocabulary in real time.

AN

Asser Ndumba

UX & Accessibility Research

Grounds the project in how a learner would actually experience wearing and using the device day to day.

PS

Protasius Simpire

Policy & Partnerships
