    How it works

    1

    Capture

    A miniature front‑facing camera continuously observes hand shape, motion and position within the wearer's field of view.

    2

    Recognise

    An on‑device gesture‑recognition model matches the motion against a basic sign vocabulary (e.g. Hello, Thank you, Yes, No, Help).

    3

    Translate

    Recognised signs are mapped to short phrases of natural language, ready to be spoken or displayed.

    4

    Speak & Display

    A text‑to‑speech module speaks the phrase aloud through a bone‑conduction speaker, while a subtle HUD caption reinforces it visually.

    Conceptual prototype disclaimer: This is an early‑stage, non‑commercial concept for research and educational demonstration purposes only. It recognises a small, fixed vocabulary of basic signs and is not a substitute for a qualified, professional sign‑language interpreter or a validated assistive device.
