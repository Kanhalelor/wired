Vision & mission

We didn't invent the goal. Namibia already set it — we're building toward it.

SignSight is our team's attempt to translate national policy commitments into something a learner can actually wear into class on a Monday morning.

Our vision

A Namibia where a learner's first language of the hands is never a barrier to belonging in a mainstream classroom — where inclusion is felt in real time, sign by sign, not only written into policy.

Our mission

To design and pilot low-cost, on-device assistive technology — starting with SignSight — that helps Namibian schools deliver on their own commitment to equitable, inclusive, quality education for every learner, regardless of hearing status.

Policy alignment

Four commitments already on the books. Here's how SignSight tries to serve them.

We are a student engineering team, not policy authors — the summaries below reflect our understanding of the spirit and stated direction of each policy, in our own words, as the foundation we're designing against.

Sector Policy on
Inclusive Education
(2013)

Valuing diversity, in the least restrictive environment

Namibia's inclusive education policy calls for valuing diversity through education, moving educationally marginalised learners toward mainstream schools wherever possible, and supporting them in the least restrictive environment available.

How SignSight aligns: a wearable communication aid is, by design, a least-restrictive-environment tool — it lets a Deaf or hard-of-hearing learner stay in the ordinary classroom, alongside hearing peers, rather than being routed to a separate setting purely for lack of real-time communication support.
Basic Education
Act (2020)

Equitable, inclusive, quality education for all

The Basic Education Act frames access to equitable, inclusive, quality education as a matter of law, not just good intention, for every learner in the Namibian system.

How SignSight aligns: the project treats "equitable access to the lesson itself" as the unit of measurement — not just enrolment or physical presence in the classroom, but whether the content of a lesson genuinely reaches every learner in it.
Namibia
Vision 2030

A prosperous, industrialised, knowledge-based nation — for everyone

Vision 2030 envisions Namibia as a healthy, knowledge-based, and prosperous nation, built on the full participation of its people.

How SignSight aligns: a knowledge-based nation depends on every learner being able to actually access knowledge as it's delivered. Locally built assistive technology also speaks to Vision 2030's ambitions around Namibian-grown innovation and technological capability.
National Policy
on Disability

Access, equity and participation as guiding principles

Namibia's disability policy framework centres access, equity, and full participation in society for persons with disabilities, including in education.

How SignSight aligns: the project's design principles — on-device privacy, open-ear audio, an everyday form factor — were chosen specifically so the technology supports dignity and participation, rather than singling a learner out.

"Leave no learner behind" only means something if it holds up sign by sign, lesson by lesson — not just at the level of enrolment statistics.

We see SignSight as a complement to trained interpreters and Deaf education specialists, not a replacement for them — a way to extend real-time communication support to the many moments a human interpreter simply cannot be present for.
